# Writing and copy

Match the audience, channel, tone and length requested. Preserve factual claims and the writer's voice; use only supplied evidence for credentials, metrics and affiliations.

For prose cleanup, remove inflated claims, filler and formulaic phrasing without erasing deliberate style. For bios and headlines, express the relevant role, domain and substantiated proof points concisely. Offer alternatives when requested or when distinct positioning choices would be useful.

Return the finished text in the requested format. Check explicit length constraints and flag unresolved factual ambiguity only when it matters.

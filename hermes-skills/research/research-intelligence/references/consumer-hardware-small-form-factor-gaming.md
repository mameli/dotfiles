# Consumer hardware note: small-form-factor gaming PCs vs console-like PCs

Use this as a compact reference when answering future current-hardware comparison questions like “can I beat a Steam Machine for ~€1100 in a small form factor?”

## Decision frame

For gaming hardware recommendations, separate the user's priorities before ranking options:

- **FPS per euro / gaming-first**: favor SFF or mini-ITX PCs with a discrete GPU.
- **Console-like simplicity / tiny footprint**: favor integrated APU systems only if the user accepts lower performance per euro.
- **Linux/SteamOS friendliness**: mention Bazzite/SteamOS-like setups and driver maturity, but do not assume they outweigh raw GPU value.
- **Noise, power, aesthetics, warranty/support**: explicitly call out when the user is paying for these rather than frame rate.

## Current pattern observed in June 2026 research

- Framework Desktop with **AMD Ryzen AI Max 385 / Radeon 8050S** is attractive as a compact 4.5L, Linux-friendly, APU-only machine.
- Treat it as a good **1080p high/medium-high** and sometimes **1440p medium/FSR** gaming box, not as the best value gaming build.
- For ~€1100, a compact PC with a **discrete GPU** generally gives better gaming performance than an APU-only Framework Desktop.
- Strix Halo mini PCs using **Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Radeon 8060S** exist (e.g. GMKtec EVO-X2, Minisforum MS-S1 Max), but European pricing observed was roughly **€1950–2700+**, making them poor gaming-only value versus SFF + discrete GPU.
- Mini PCs with ordinary APUs like HX 370 / Radeon 890M are good general-purpose boxes but are not strong replacements for a gaming-focused Steam Machine-class device.

## Answer shape that worked

Use a short recommendation table:

| Option | Choose if… | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| SFF/mini-ITX + discrete GPU | gaming performance per euro matters most | best choice |
| Framework Desktop AI Max 385 | tiny, polished, Linux-friendly, APU-only matters | good but expensive for gaming |
| Strix Halo Max+ 395 mini PC | AI/LLM/workstation plus gaming, high budget | interesting but usually over budget |
| HX 370/890M mini PC | lower-cost light gaming/general PC | not a Steam Machine replacement |

## Verification checklist

- Check current EU pricing, not just US MSRP.
- Distinguish leaked/pre-release benchmarks from final reviews.
- Cite exact chip/GPU names (e.g. Radeon 8050S vs 8060S vs 890M) because performance tiers differ sharply.
- Avoid overclaiming “better than Steam Machine” unless both final price and final GPU benchmarks are available.
